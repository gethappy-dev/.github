### Happy Solutions

AI-native software studio for startups. One senior engineer leads your build, AI writes much of the code, and every feature is tested.

**Services**
- **MVP Sprint**: a tested MVP live with real users in about 4 weeks, fixed price
- **Product Retainer**: a senior engineer and AI working through your backlog for a monthly fee
- **Rescue and Harden**: production audit and fixes for apps built with AI tools
- **Maintain and Run**: hosting, monitoring, updates, automated tests and CI/CD

**How we test every feature**
Acceptance criteria first, tests before code, automated checks on every change, security checks, manual QA, and your sign-off.

Client projects live in private repositories, transferred to the client at handover.
